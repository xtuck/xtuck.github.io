Privacy Policy

How Quiet Bloom handles your data.

Quiet Bloom is a digital coloring book. Artwork progress is stored on your device. We use Apple services for subscriptions and Google Mobile Ads for advertising in the free version.

Effective date: May 25, 2026 Contact: gxtuna@gmail.com

Local progress

Your coloring progress and local unlock state are stored on your device.

Ads

The free version may request ads through Google Mobile Ads and related consent tools.

Subscriptions

Quiet Bloom Plus purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

1. Information We Store Locally

Quiet Bloom stores artwork progress, selected colors, local ad unlocks, settings, and local membership cache on your device. This information is used to restore your experience when you return to the app.

2. Advertising and Consent

The free version may show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Ads may be served by Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your region and system settings, Google User Messaging Platform may ask for privacy choices before ad requests are made.

Rewarded ads are only shown after you choose to unlock an eligible coloring page. Plus removes forced banner and interstitial ads.

3. Purchases

Quiet Bloom Plus subscriptions are purchased through your App Store account. Apple processes payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund handling. The app may read subscription status from Apple in order to unlock Plus benefits.

4. Photos and Sharing

If you choose to save or share artwork, Quiet Bloom may use system sharing or photo library features. The app does not upload your artwork to our own server.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for subscriptions and purchase status.
  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising in the free version.
  • Google User Messaging Platform for ad privacy choices where required.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at gxtuna@gmail.com.
