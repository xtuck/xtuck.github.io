Privacy Policy

How Quiet Bloom handles your data.

Quiet Bloom is a calm coloring book. Artwork progress is stored on your device. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and Quiet Bloom does not collect data from the app.

Effective date: August 25, 2026 Contact: gxtuna@gmail.com

Local progress

Your coloring progress and local unlock state are stored on your device.

No data collection

There are no ads, analytics, tracking, or Quiet Bloom account services.

Subscriptions

Quiet Bloom Plus purchases are processed through the App Store or Google Play.

1. Information We Store Locally

Quiet Bloom stores artwork progress, selected colors, settings, favorites, and a local membership cache on your device. This information is used to restore your experience when you return to the app. It is not uploaded to a Quiet Bloom server.

2. Data Collection and Tracking

Quiet Bloom does not collect data from the app. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not operate a user account or cloud-sync service, and does not track you across apps or websites.

The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access or transmit IDFA.

3. Purchases

Quiet Bloom Plus subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund handling for purchases made through its store. The app may read the corresponding subscription status in order to unlock Plus benefits.

4. Photos and Sharing

If you choose to save or share artwork, Quiet Bloom uses system sharing or media-library features. On Android 10 and later, saving an image created by the app does not require access to your entire photo library. Android 9 and earlier may request legacy storage write permission only when you save. iOS may request permission to add images to Photos. The app does not upload your artwork to our own server.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for iOS subscriptions and purchase status.
  • Google Play and Google Play Billing for Android subscriptions and purchase status.
  • Apple or Google system services when you choose to save, share, or review the app.

These platform services process information under the applicable provider's terms and privacy policy. Quiet Bloom does not receive your full payment details.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at gxtuna@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as Quiet Bloom changes. The latest version will be available at this URL.
